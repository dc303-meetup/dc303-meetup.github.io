{%- comment -%} Resolves where and when one meetup happens, falling back to site.venue / site.meeting. Sets, for the caller to read: venue_name, venue_address, venue_map, meetup_hours. Liquid's `assign` writes to the outermost scope, so these survive the include; this is the one place the venue defaults are applied. `location:` front matter may be either a mapping (name / address / map, each optional and independently defaulted) or a plain string, which is treated as the whole address. Params: post {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign loc = include.post.location -%} {%- if loc and loc.name == nil and loc.address == nil and loc.map == nil -%} {%- assign venue_name = nil -%} {%- assign venue_address = loc -%} {%- assign venue_map = nil -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign venue_name = loc.name | default: site.venue.name -%} {%- assign venue_address = loc.address | default: site.venue.address -%} {%- assign venue_map = loc.map | default: site.venue.map -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign meetup_hours = include.post.time | default: site.meeting.time -%}
