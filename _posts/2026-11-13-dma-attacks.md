John Hoopes (tilver) will present DMA attacks, building mainly on Ulf Frisk’s work. He’ll demo attacks against Linux and Windows, cover ATM security and recent incidents, and discuss DMA against TPM-protected systems and game cheating.

Tilver has been in security for 25 years and collected a pile of esoteric anecdotes that are now being put to shame by AI agents evading their handlers. He’s a founding member of Denhac and has been with DC303 for 20 years.
