QR code phishing travels through carrier networks completely unseen - existing spam filters check text but can't see inside QR code images. My research includes 190 real live scans across 55 participants with zero hesitations, introduces the Signal Switch behavioral framework, and includes a live audience demo where attendees scan a real QR code and experience the attack firsthand.
